### Layer 0: CFTN (Computational Foundation Truth Network)

**What it is:**
- Root cryptographic & mathematical primitives
- Ensures all higher layers comply with computational truth
- Validates all state transitions, transforms, and assertions

**Status:** ✅ Complete (imported)

### Layer 1: ZFTN (Zero Forward Truth Network)

**What it is:**
- Multi-agent sandbox engine
- Routes messages through 9 coordinated agents
- Each agent has a specialized role
- All outputs validated through SupervisorGate

**The 9 Agents:**

1. **PlannerAgent** — Generates strategic plans from raw input
2. **CoderAgent** — Drafts C# implementations (requires SupervisorGate approval)
3. **ArchitectAgent** — Designs system architecture and structure
4. **CriticAgent** — Evaluates work, identifies issues, suggests improvements
5. **ArchivistAgent** — Manages project memory, history, and provenance
6. **ValidatorAgent** — Applies ZFTN truth validation rules
7. **NHIAgent** — Applies Non-Human Intelligence symbolic perspective
8. **NonHumanAIAgent** — Non-human logic synthesis and reasoning
9. **HumanIntentAgent** — Ensures outputs align with human values & family mission

**Status:** 🔨 Foundation sandbox in development (THIS IS NOW)

## What the Sandbox Does

The **ZFTN Sandbox** is the **organizing & synthesizing engine** that:

1. **Ingests** all project components
   - CFTN truth primitives
   - Truth-or-Dare prompts & behavioral data
   - Cornerstone cinematic facts & narratives
   - Family mission statements

2. **Routes through agents**
   - Planner thinks strategically
   - Coder implements solutions
   - Architect designs coherence
   - Critic evaluates quality
   - Archivist maintains memory
   - Validator ensures truth compliance
   - NHI & NonHumanAI provide non-human perspective
   - HumanIntent keeps it family-aligned

3. **Validates through SupervisorGate**
   - All high-impact outputs require validation
   - Approval log maintained for audit trail
   - Truth compliance enforced

4. **Outputs integrated synthesis**
   - Organized narrative
   - Validated prompts
   - Coherent cinematics
   - Truth-bearing code
